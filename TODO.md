| TF-30 | Cover shadow features — Lane Control pass blocking + blocking score | Wicked–Monstah | Cascioli, Wang, Stradiotti, Van Roy, Robberechts, Wouters, Jaspers & Davis 2025 ([Hudl/DTAI paper](https://static.hudl.com/craft/performance-insights-research-stage/2025/Quantifying-Off-Ball-Defensive-Impact-through-Cover-Shadows-Lorenzo-Casciolio-Allen-Wang.pdf)); extends Bekkers 2024 TTI ([arXiv:2501.04712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04712)), Spearman 2017 pass physics | **New defensive feature category — lane-specific pass obstruction (off-ball defensive value).** The paper (same DTAI/KU Leuven group as VAEP) introduces Lane Control, a physics-based model for detecting whether a defender is blocking a passing lane, validated on 69,500 progressive passes across Big Five 2024/25 (11–15% balanced accuracy improvement over geometric baselines). Three pieces: **(a) `lane_control(frames, passer_xy, receiver_xy)`** → per-defender pass-blocking probability via corridor-discretized TTI race. Discretizes the passing corridor into center + left/right edge lines × 30 sample points; computes TTI for ball (drag-based flight), defenders (accelerate-then-cruise + reaction delay), and attackers at each point; sigmoid-based probability conversion → `P_blocked` vs `P_received` per line; three decision rules (Any/Majority/All lines blocked). Extends existing `_bekkers_tti()` kernel in `_kernels.py` from player-to-player to player-to-lane-point — core TTI infrastructure already shipped. **(b) `blocking_score(frames, actions)`** → per-defender threat reduction via counterfactual removal. `blocking_score = threat(with_defender) − threat(without_defender)`. Remove lane-blocking defenders from snapshot, recompute threat surface. Paper uses xT × SoccerMap(pass_selection) × SoccerMap(pass_success); silly-kicks can substitute xT grid (shipped in `xthreat.py`) or pitch-control-weighted variant (TF-7, shipped) for the threat layer — avoids needing a CNN architecture. Positive blocking_score = defender actively suppressing attacking value. Paper example: cover shadows reduced threat by ~25% in typical snapshot. **(c) Action-coupled `cover_shadow_at_action` + `add_cover_shadows` aggregator + `cover_shadow_xfns` VAEP factory.** Pattern follows existing per-Series tracking-aware features (ADR-005). Paper also includes a defensive positioning optimization (Hungarian algorithm on cone-intersection polygons — 789/822 dangerous passes become blockable with small repositioning); defer optimization to future "what-if" coaching tool, not a per-frame VAEP feature. **Complements existing features:** pressure (TF-2) measures proximity-based influence on ball carrier; cover shadows measure lane-blocking influence on passing options — different defensive mechanisms. Off-ball runs (TF-4) identify attacking movement; cover shadows identify the defensive response nullifying it. Defensive line (TF-14) provides the "attackers between ball and goal" geometry the paper uses to filter dangerous receivers. **GKDV relevance:** GK-specific blocking_score (GK obstructing shot/cross lanes) is a natural TF-15 primitive; `blocking_score(actual_GK) − blocking_score(ghost_GK)` provides lane-specific deterrent signal for TF-19. **Empirical tuning needed:** corridor width, TTI sigmoid parameters (paper calibrated on StatsBomb 360 freeze frames; silly-kicks has full tracking → higher-fidelity velocity/acceleration, but parameter transfer needs validation). **Don't implement SoccerMap** — use xT or pitch-control surface as threat layer. Depends on: TF-7 (shipped), TF-5 (shipped), TF-14 (shipped). ~300–400 LOC. |

**GKDV research program (TF-15..TF-19).** TF-15 through TF-19 form a coherent research arc towards the **GK Deterrent Value** metric — a per-frame measure of how much the defending goalkeeper's actual position depresses opponent attempt-probabilities (shot / cross / key pass) relative to a league-average "ghost" GK in the same frame state. Origin: 2026-05-01 deterrent-effect investigation; closes the published-literature gap that no GK-evaluation framework today (StatsBomb four-pillar, Lamberts GVM, Sloan SAC data-driven framework, Anzer & Bauer xGOT, Driblab Goals Prevented) measures positioning-as-deterrent. **Foundation:** TF-7 (pitch control) + TF-13 (frame-based GK ID) + TF-14 (defensive-line geometry). **Layer 1:** TF-15 (threat-weighted GK influence primitives). **Layer 2:** TF-16 / TF-17 / TF-18 (decision-probability surfaces for shot-occurrence and cross-attempt + ghost-GK regression). **Layer 3:** TF-19 (composition + validation harness). **Sequencing:** TF-15 lands first as a self-contained publishable primitive; TF-16/17/18 can be parallelised (TF-18's regression baseline is the most spec-complete of the trio, TF-17's GK-confounder extension is the most research-heavy); TF-19 is two PR cycles and gets its own ADR. **External complements:** DAS (Bischofberger & Baca 2026, `accessible-space` on PyPI; TF-28, shipped 3.8.0) provides physics-based counterfactual space valuation; cover shadows (Cascioli et al. 2025; see TF-30) provide lane-specific pass-obstruction metrics. Both can serve as independent cross-checks on the probability-surface decomposition in TF-19.
