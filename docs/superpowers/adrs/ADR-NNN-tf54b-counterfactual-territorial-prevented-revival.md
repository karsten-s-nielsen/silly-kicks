## Context

TF-54 v1 (4.108.0, ADR-086) shipped `silly_kicks.territory.compute_territorial_dominance` -- the "Van Dijk" territorial-dominance lens (Sumpter, *Soccermatics* / Twelve module 10.2) -- with a pluggable `method=` family and a **reserved typed door** `method="counterfactual"` that raised `NotImplementedError`. v1's default (`completed_failed`) values every opponent pass whose recorded SPADL `end` lands inside a defender's trimmed hull at `xT(end)`: conceded if completed, prevented if failed. That "prevented" number is a proxy -- *"threat that died in the territory"* -- because a **failed** pass's SPADL `end` is the death/recovery location (`_derive_end_coordinates` = the next action's start), not the pass's intended target. A pass aimed into the territory but intercepted before its recorded death lands outside the hull and is never counted -- a documented under-count in the v1 impl review and docstring.

TF-54b closes that door: model the *counterfactual* threat a defender's positioning prevented -- what the aimed-in pass would have been worth had it reached its intended target -- as a proper expected-minus-realized quantity, the same GSAA idiom the house shipped for goalkeepers in TF-59 (`goals_prevented = ΣPSxG - GA`, ADR-085).

**The load-bearing design question, resolved by spec review (SPEC-01/-06):** an "expected" threat needs a completion probability, and neither of the two available xT families supplies one usably. `singh_counts`'s transition matrix gives only a tiny per-*zone* marginal (~0.01-0.1), which collapses the whole headline to `-conceded`; `kde_smoothed`'s transition matrix is row-stochastic and carries *no* completion signal at all. A real per-pass `P_complete` (~0.2-0.9) needs a fitted model that silly-kicks did not have. This is the reason the cycle also ships a new reusable `expected_passing.PassCompletionModel` rather than trying to squeeze completion out of the injected xT.

**The load-bearing scoping question:** an event-only counterfactual defensive valuation is structurally the same problem as GKDV/ghosting, and xt-gk-v2 (a possession-value ghosting surface) was shipped, failed its out-of-sample construct-validity gate, and was quarantined (`docs/research/xtgk_v2_construct_validity/`). TF-54b is judged different because **every component here has real ground truth**: the completion model validates on observed completed/failed outcomes (held-out AUC/ECE/Brier), the target-recovery model (`q`) validates via a synthetic-interception substrate with a known hidden true end, and the composed metric runs a pre-registered battery (mechanism validity + a locked elite-defender prior as primary gates; reliability/discriminant/outcome-lens as reported secondary). "Gold standard" requires both a principled construct AND a validated one -- so the method ships **available but non-default**, with the validation report attached, and promotion to default is a deliberately separate decision.

### Revival context (deltas beyond the R2-approved baseline)

**Why the door has to be RE-ADDED (Chesterton's Fence).** In the cycles between the baseline design and this
revival, the `counterfactual` door was **removed** on `main`: ADR-090 gave the *name* "counterfactual" to
the tracking-consuming `silly_kicks.territorial_defense` sibling, and when the reusable seams
(`expected_passing.PassCompletionModel`, `xthreat.destination_profiles`, the trimmed hull) were carried
onto `main`, the event-only cone consumer itself was deliberately **not** carried. Today
`TERRITORY_METHODS == frozenset({"completed_failed"})` and `compute_territorial_dominance` carries no
`completion_model` parameter. The door was removed because it had degenerated to a dead
`NotImplementedError` after the name was taken -- **NOT** because the cone mechanism was rejected. The
event-only cone is a *distinct mechanism* (no pitch control) and **coexists** with `territorial_defense`
under the TF-54b family. This ADR records that re-add explicitly so a future reader does not read it as
reverting ADR-090.

**Empirical status, stated honestly.** The cone mechanism reached spec-review **R2 APPROVE** -- a *design*
verdict. It was **never merged and never run**; there is no
`docs/research/territory_counterfactual_construct_validity/` on `main` (only the SB360 *sibling*
`territorial_defense_construct_validity/` exists). So no empirical prior supports the headline. Because
`territory_xt_conceded` is formula-identical to v1, the composed "beats v1" margin lives ENTIRELY in the
failed-pass (prevented) leg, and a **null result is a real, un-gated risk** -- this cycle's validation must
be able to *report a null* (reported-not-gated; the default never flips on a null).

## Decision

Ship the counterfactual valuation (`method="counterfactual"`) in `silly_kicks.territory`, re-adding the removed door and filling it with the real event-only implementation, in **one combined cycle** with the broad-corpus re-fit of the `silly_kicks.expected_passing.PassCompletionModel` it depends on (both co-validated rather than split across PRs).

1. **The quantity: Option D, the completion-weighted expected-minus-realized counterfactual.** For every opponent pass aimed into a defender's trimmed hull: `expected_threat = P_complete(target) * xT(target)`, `realized_threat = xT(target)` if completed else `0`, and the GSAA-style headline `xt_prevented_above_expectation = expected - realized`. A **completed** pass is valued at its observed end (no integration needed: `expected = c(origin,end)*xT(end)`, `realized = xT(end)`). A **failed** pass's target is unobserved, so it is modeled as an integral over a target distribution `q`: `expected = Σ_z q(z) * c(origin,z) * xT(z)`, `z` ranging over the xT-grid zones whose centres fall inside the defender's hull AND inside a cone of half-angle `direction_cone_degrees` around the pass's observed death direction from its origin. Completion was explicitly **not dropped** (owner ruling: dropping it would let "expected" assume every aimed pass completes, over-crediting) and **not sourced from the injected xT's transition matrix** (SPEC-01/-06, above) -- it comes from the reusable `PassCompletionModel`, evaluated at each hypothesized target `z`.
2. **The joint model has three components, each independently sourced and independently validated.** Target distribution `q` -- the injected xT's transition mass, restricted to the death-direction cone and renormalized, consumed ONLY as a family-agnostic distribution (never a row-mass, so both `singh_counts` and `kde_smoothed` are valid). Completion `c` -- the `PassCompletionModel`, evaluated at the hypothesized target (not at the truncated death geometry, which would bias `c` low exactly on the passes that failed; not origin-only, which cannot distinguish a short from a long intended pass). Value `xT(z)` -- the same injected, fitted `ExpectedThreat` v1 already required. A pass whose cone-and-hull intersection carries transition support below `CounterfactualParams.min_transition_support` (including the cone-misses-the-hull case) is an unresolvable target: dropped and counted (`territory_target_source="unresolved"`, `TerritoryReport.n_target_unresolved`), never a fabricated zero (ADR-042).
3. **The public `xthreat` seam owns the flat-index / y-inversion convention.** `destination_profiles(model, origin_x, origin_y) -> DestinationProfile` (`zone_centres`, `zone_values`, raw per-origin `probabilities` transition rows, all in physical SPADL coordinates) is the only way `territory` touches xT's transition structure -- never the raw, flat-indexed, y-inverted `.transition_matrix` / `.xT` (the ADR-041 defect class one module over). The seam is family-agnostic by construction (a distribution, not a row-mass), so no fail-closed guard on which xT method is injected is needed. **The seam is already on `main`** (carried from the baseline branch); the revival adds the `territory`-side consumer, not the seam.
4. **Method-dependent schema, `completed_failed` byte-identical (SPEC-04).** `columns_for_method(method)` resolves the output schema: `"completed_failed"` returns exactly the v1 schema, unchanged; `"counterfactual"` returns that same schema plus five new columns (`territory_expected_threat_faced`, `territory_xt_prevented_above_expectation`, `territory_passes_aimed_into_hull`, `territory_mean_completion_faced`, `territory_target_source`). The four METRIC columns are glossaried (`emitting_module="silly_kicks.territory._counterfactual"`, each with `higher_is_better`); `territory_target_source` is PROVENANCE and is deliberately NOT glossaried, mirroring `territory_hull_source`. Membership is pinned so `territory_xt_conceded` stays comparable across methods: a completed pass counts iff its observed end is point-in-hull (the v1 rule, unchanged); a failed pass counts iff its death-direction cone intersects the hull region (the re-opened "aimed-in-died-short" case v1 could not see). Rate-column denominators are documented as method-dependent (`completed_failed` divides by `passes_into_hull`; `counterfactual` divides by `passes_aimed_into_hull`).
5. **The reusable `PassCompletionModel` (`silly_kicks.expected_passing`) is RE-FIT and RE-BUNDLED on the broad public open-data corpus (delta -- owner decision 2026-09-17).** Event-only logistic regression -- `sigmoid(standardize(X) @ coef + intercept)` served in pure numpy (sklearn imported only inside `fit`) -- following the house bundled-trained-artifact discipline verbatim (ADR-011/016/040/044/050): pickle-free JSON + `SHA256SUMS`, feature contract, chirality probe, fail-closed `load()`, imports `spadl` / `id_compat` / numpy ONLY (never `tracking`, AST import-allowlist gate). The MODEL ITSELF is unchanged and is already on `main`. This cycle re-fits it on the **full StatsBomb open-data corpus** (`scripts/_sb_open_data.all_open_competitions()` -- ~3,961 matches at the reference run, re-counted at run time), replacing the WC2022-trained bundled weights already on `main`. Rationale: a broader default is a better default and matches the substrate the metric is validated on. Held-out AUC/ECE/Brier (GroupKFold-by-match) must clear the pre-registered floors or the re-bundle does not ship (fall back to the existing bundled weights, recorded). The re-fit is owner-run at Commit 1 (clean tree, `--out` outside the repo); `training_commit` = Commit 1; the model card records the corpus change.
6. **Validation on the BROAD corpus, in-cycle, owner-run, reported-not-gated (delta 2).** Validation and re-bundle run on the full open-data corpus, **not** WC2022-only, because the broad corpus supplies the failed-pass-into-hull volume, geometry diversity, and multi-season club structure the metric and the ranking census need. Every component has real ground truth: `PassCompletionModel` on held-out AUC/ECE/Brier-skill; `q`'s target recovery on a synthetic-interception substrate that perturbs a completed pass's true end in BOTH distance and angle. The composed metric's PRIMARY gate is two-part -- mechanism validity (the composed counterfactual beats both naive baselines) AND a locked, pre-registered elite-defender ("Van Dijk") prior list re-drawn for the broad corpus (the TF-19 `NAMED_KEEPER_PRIOR` idiom, committed before the owner run). Reliability, discriminant, and an outcome-lens are reported but explicitly non-gating. **Promotion of `counterfactual` to the default method is a separate, follow-on ADR-009 apply decision**, made after the owner reads the validation report -- not a decision this ADR makes. What the broad corpus buys is POWER and a better-fit model; it does NOT guarantee a positive margin, and a null is reportable.
7. **Defender-ranking census + crossed-ICC gate (delta 4 -- the new deliverable).** The per-`(defender, match)` metric is team-conditioned by construction, so a defender **ranking** is licensed only if the corpus can separate the defender-intrinsic component from the team component -- a crossed **defender+team** intraclass-correlation (ICC) identifiability question (the ADR-090 posture, made testable rather than assumed). `scripts/build_territory_ranking_census.py` (event-only, `for_each`-sharded, provenance-stamped) runs two tiers with LOCKED pre-registered thresholds committed in Commit 1: **Tier 1** counts distinct defenders, defenders appearing on ≥2 distinct teams (the transfer-driven crossed cells), and defender×team cells clearing a `min_passes_faced` floor (always runs, always reported); **Tier 2** (iff Tier 1 clears) is a pure-numpy crossed random-effects variance-components estimate of the defender-intrinsic ICC net of team + a bootstrap power leg (both NEW work -- `causal.power` ships only `att_power_curve`, there is no `icc_power_curve` in the library). **Decision rule (owner-approved 2026-09-17): if the gate clears, publish a REPORTED ranking artifact in-cycle (top defenders by `xt_prevented_above_expectation` at adequate pass-faced volume, with the crossed-ICC evidence) AND record an ADR-009 apply that the metric is player-attributable ON THIS CORPUS -- this is an ADR-009 apply, NOT a new library ranking API; the per-`(defender, match)` metric columns and the library surface do not change. If the gate fails, the cycle ships the metric only and the census reports "ranking not licensed on this corpus" with the measured counts/ICC/power.** Either way the census runs, is reported, and conserves its counts (ADR-042).
8. **The `expected_passing` C4 container is already modelled; the 33 action-coupled `add_*` aggregator count is unchanged** -- neither the counterfactual method, the completion model, nor the census is an `add_*`.
9. **Per-provider params ship EMPTY** (ADR-009) -- `CounterfactualParams.for_provider` and `TerritoryParams.for_provider` resolve to the base config until a separate gated apply-PR.
10. **The bundled `PassCompletionModel` is IN-REPO (not HF-published) and carries a `MODEL_CARD.md`.** It is the same event-only-logistic class as `gk_completion` / `receiver` / `retention`, all of which bundle their weights in-repo with a card. `scripts/train_pass_completion.py` **generates** the card from the out-of-fold metrics and writes it next to the weights, satisfying ADR-088 for the in-repo path. The card records the broad-corpus re-fit.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Drop the completion weight (value only the intended target, unweighted) | A de-scope: "expected" then silently assumes every aimed pass completes, over-crediting every defender's prevented total. Owner ruling: do the completion weight properly. |
| Source `P_complete` from the injected xT's transition matrix `T` | SPEC-06: `singh_counts`'s per-zone marginal is ~0.01-0.1, collapsing the headline to `-conceded`. SPEC-01: `kde_smoothed`'s `T` is row-stochastic and carries no completion signal at all. A per-pass `P_complete` needs a fitted model. |
| Fail-closed on a non-`singh_counts` xT (restrict the door to one xT family) | Unnecessary once completion is its own model: the injected `T` is consumed ONLY as a family-agnostic destination distribution, valid for both families. |
| Evaluate `c` at the death geometry, or origin-only | Death geometry is truncated -- it biases `c` low exactly on the passes that failed. Origin-only cannot distinguish a short intended pass from a long one. Evaluate `c` at the hypothesized target `z`, integrated over `q`. |
| A possession-value ghosting surface (`V(z,p)`, the xt-gk-v2 construct) | Its construct failed its own out-of-sample validity gate and was quarantined; reusing it here would duplicate a known-bad pattern. Reserved as a future target-valuator swap, not this cycle's headline. |
| Split into a completion-model PR and a territory-counterfactual PR | The two are co-designed and co-validated; splitting would ship an unvalidated intermediate. |
| Same output columns for both methods; only the values change | Loses the "expected faced" / "above expectation" names entirely under `completed_failed`, and conflates two different quantities under one column name. A method-dependent schema keeps the new quantities explicit while preserving `completed_failed` byte-identity. |
| **Leave the removed door removed / re-open the cone mechanism DESIGN** | The door was removed only because it was a dead `NotImplementedError` after ADR-090 took the name -- not a rejection of the cone. The mechanism reached R2 APPROVE (a design verdict); the revival treats the *design* as settled and spends its review budget on the deltas. Its empirical validity is what THIS cycle produces (a null is reportable). |
| **Restrict validation / re-fit to WC2022** (the baseline's public leg) | The point of the revival is broad-corpus: the ~3,961 open matches give the multi-season club-league structure the ranking census needs; WC2022-only forecloses the ranking by construction and gives a worse default model. |
| **Keep the WC2022-trained bundled weights** | Owner decision 2026-09-17: re-fit on the broad corpus (better default; validated substrate == serving substrate). |
| **Ship a defender ranking unconditionally** | The player-vs-team confound is a statistical-validity constraint, not a scope cut. Gate the ranking on the crossed-cell census. |
| **Add a ranking function to the library** | ADR-009 keeps rankings consumer-side; the library ships the per-`(defender, match)` primitive, the ranking is a REPORTED artifact + an ADR-009 apply. |

## Consequences

### Positive
- Closes TF-54 v1's documented under-count: a pass aimed into a defender's territory but intercepted short of its recorded death location is now counted, valued at its modeled intended target rather than silently dropped.
- Re-adds a mechanism that was removed for an incidental reason (name collision), not on merit -- Chesterton's Fence discharged: the removed door was a dead `NotImplementedError`, the cone coexists with the ADR-090 tracking sibling.
- The reusable, event-only `PassCompletionModel` is re-fit on a broad public open-data corpus -- a better default that matches the validation substrate -- and gains a SECOND live consumer (the TF-62 GK-decision reconstruction tiers already inject it; the territorial cone now does too).
- Every component of the composed metric has independently verifiable ground truth (unlike the quarantined xt-gk-v2 surface), so the validation report is a real construct-validity check.
- The defender-ranking census makes the ADR-090 player-vs-team-confound posture *testable on measured evidence* rather than assumed: a ranking ships in-cycle only if the crossed-ICC gate clears, else the census reports "not licensed on this corpus".
- Fully additive: `method="completed_failed"` -- the default -- is untouched in shape and value; no VAEP/tracking retrain, no re-materialize.

### Negative
- `q`'s target model has one irreducible event-only assumption: a failed pass's intended direction is approximated by its (noisy) observed death direction, spread over a cone rather than trusted as a ray -- named in the docstring, the spec, and the validation report, not silently assumed away.
- The counterfactual method's failed-pass valuation evaluates `PassCompletionModel` at hypothesized targets it never observed as a specific pass's actual origin-target pair -- an interpolation within the model's trained geometry range, not extrapolation, but still an assumption resting on the completion model's held-out validation across that range.
- The composed "beats v1" margin lives ENTIRELY in the failed-pass leg (`territory_xt_conceded` is v1-identical), so a null result is a real, un-gated risk; the cycle's validation must be able to report it and the default never flips on a null.
- The method ships **available and non-default** until the owner reads the in-cycle validation report; a team consuming `silly_kicks.territory` today gets no behavior change and must opt in explicitly.
- A defender ranking is licensed ONLY if the crossed-cell census clears; even on the broad corpus the defender-vs-team confound may be unidentifiable, in which case the ranking stays a further ADR-009 gated on a larger multi-club transfer corpus.
- Two commits are required (code, then the re-bundled weights + validation + census artifacts) because committing either with the other would force a dirty-tree provenance stamp on a cited artifact -- load-bearing release-process overhead, not incidental.

### Neutral
- `CounterfactualParams.for_provider` is empty scaffolding, mirroring `TerritoryParams`, until a future ADR-009 calibration apply-gate.
- Promotion of `counterfactual` to the default `method=` value is explicitly out of scope for this ADR and is deferred to a follow-on decision.
- Coexistence: the two TF-54b siblings -- the event-only cone (`silly_kicks.territory._counterfactual`, this ADR) and the tracking SB360 removal counterfactual (`silly_kicks.territorial_defense`, ADR-090, demoted to experimental) -- are distinct mechanisms under one family name and do not import each other.
