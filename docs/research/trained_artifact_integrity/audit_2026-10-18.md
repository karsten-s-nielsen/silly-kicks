## Why xShot is the defect (both tiers)

The `type_name`-presence guard was bypassed on the pining full-actions table, so the label counted **any**
action within 1 s, not just shots. Physical maximum for an occurrence label is `n_events · fps · horizon /
n_rows`; for the 17-match wheel corpus (389 shots, 10 fps, 1 s) the max positive rate is ≈ 0.025 — the
committed 22.2% is ~9× that (the HF sc_extended tier: 18.9%, same bypass). The corrected `type_id` filter
yields 1.54% (wheel) / 1.34% (sc_extended), within the band. The committed `pr_auc` (0.34 / 0.585) measured
a trivial high-base-rate task; the fresh pr_auc (0.08 / 0.12) scores the real shot-occurrence task — lower
in absolute terms **because the old figure measured the wrong label, not a quality regression**. xCross used
the correct `type_id` filter and was only velocity-stale.
